# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Recruiters, engineering leads, tech companies, and prospective clients looking to hire a highly skilled Full-Stack Developer.

## Product Purpose
To showcase Juathan Coelho Duarte's (Juats) professional experience, skills, and projects in a high-craft, fast, accessible, and premium presentation, encouraging visitors to connect or hire him.

## Brand Personality
Precise, technical, minimalist, high-craft. Evokes confidence, clarity, and professionalism.

## Anti-references
Neon gradient templates, over-animated floating canvas backgrounds, glassmorphism SaaS templates, double-nested bordered boxes.

## Design Principles
1. **Content First, Accent Second**: Colors and accents are used sparingly (under 10%) to draw attention to important links or active states.
2. **Typography as Structure**: Hierarchy is established through size, weight, and tracking of Manrope and JetBrains Mono, not through decorative bars or shapes.
3. **Flat Solid Surfaces**: Replaces floating gradients and glassmorphism blurs with solid light/dark spaces, crisp borders, and negative space.
4. **Purposeful Motion**: Entrance animations should feel fast and organic, never drawing attention away from readability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Target: WCAG 2.1 AA compliance.
- High contrast: Text colors maintain a >= 4.5:1 ratio against solid background fills.
- Keyboard navigability: All interactive components have clean focus states.
- Prefers-reduced-motion: Every motion effect degrades cleanly to static or simple crossfades.
